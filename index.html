ABOUT US

Built for the minutes that matter most

Traditional donor search means phone trees, hospital calls and hoping a blood bank has stock. BloodConnect replaces that with instant, location-aware matching — cutting the search from hours to minutes.

Live location matching

Donors and requests are matched using precise geo-coordinates and the Haversine distance formula — no guessing which "area" someone is in.

True compatibility, not just group

We match on real donor→recipient compatibility (e.g. O- to everyone), so a request never misses an eligible donor.

Instant alerts, first-accept wins

Nearby eligible donors are notified simultaneously; whoever accepts first is connected — no delay waiting on a single contact.

HOW IT WORKS

From request to reply in three steps

1

Register with live location

Donors sign up with their blood group and share their live location once — the app keeps a distance-ready profile on file.

2

Emergency request raised

A patient or hospital selects the required group and urgency. BloodConnect searches a 5–25 km radius instantly.

3

Simultaneous alerts sent

Every compatible, available donor nearby is notified at once. The first to accept is connected directly to the requester.
